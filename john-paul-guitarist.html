Born and rooted in Kolkata, John Paul began his professional journey around 2010 — cutting his teeth with local bands including the Bengali outfit Lakhichara, where he developed the rhythmic sensitivity that would define his later work.

His project John Paul Live is where all the threads meet: funk grooves, jazz harmonics, rock distortion, and the meditative depth of Indian classical rāgas — woven together in real time, on stage.

As a session musician, he has earned the trust of India's biggest voices — recording and performing alongside Arijit Singh, Sonu Nigam, Amit Trivedi, and Raghu Dixit. His guitar is distinctive enough to be sought, and disciplined enough to serve the song.

Kalpana
कल्पना · Imagination

A deeply personal record dedicated to John Paul's late mother, Kalpana is equal parts grief and celebration — a sonic portrait built from imagination, memory, and music.

Featuring collaborations with the legendary Shankar Mahadevan alongside a constellation of national and international artists, the album moves through genres as fluidly as John Paul himself: jazz interludes dissolving into Indian classical passages, rock crescendos settling into delicate acoustic reveries.
